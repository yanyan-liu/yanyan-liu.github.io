Acknowledgement¶

I would like to express my gratitude for everyone that has generously offered their support and help during my PhD at EPFL. First of all, I would like to thank my advisors Prof. Michael Unser and Dr. Jonathan Dong. Under their guidance, I have progressed and learned a lot over the past five years in many interesting research projects. In particular, Jonathan has not only been my academic supervisor, but also a close friend. The many hours that we have spent together on advancing different projects and attending events inside and outside EPFL have become a precious memory during my stay at the Biomedical Imaging Group. I am indebted to Michael and Jonathan for my knowledge in imaging and optics as well as the continuous support that I have received from them throughout my PhD. I would like to thank my mentor, Prof. Andrei Popescu-Belis, for his heartful support of me all these years. I would also like to thank the members in my thesis jury: Prof. Jean-Philippe Thiran, Prof. Yves Bellouard, Prof. Sylvain Gigan, and Dr. Emmanuel Soubies, for taking their time to review my work and attending my defense.

Thanks to the platform provided to me by our lab, I have had many great opportunities to collaborate with experts in Switzerland and Europe on different topics. Without their generous offer of experimental datasets or precious insights from their respective field, the work accomplished in this thesis would have not been possible. Hence, I would like to thank our collaborators: Dr. François Marelli from Idiap, Dr. Cédric Schmidt from the University of Geneva, Prof. André C. Stiel, Dr. Hailong He, and Dr. Yishu Huang from the Helmholtz Zentrum Müchen, Prof. Francisco Balzarotti and Mehrta Shirzadian from the Vienna BioCenter. I owe my knowledge about each of the three imaging modalities in my thesis to them.

My appreciation extends to Dr. Aleix Boquet I Pujadas, Dr. Daniel Sage, and Dr. Vasiliki Stergiopoulou from EPFL for the time that we have spent together on fruitful discussions over different projects, and Dr. Martin Zach for helping to proofread sections in my thesis. In particular, I would like to thank Jonathan Chuah, an expert in computational science, with whom I have had a very pleasant and productive time while working on photoacoustic imaging.

I would like to express my deep appreciation for my academic mentors along the way: Prof. Habib Ammari for the warm support and strong belief in me during my time at ETH, Prof. Wuwei Ren for introducing me to the field of optical imaging in 2018, and Prof. Hui Zhang for supporting me to pursue higher degrees in Europe during my last year at Beijing Normal University. I would have not made it so far without their guidance.

Besides research, I have shared many happy moments with my friends in the beautiful nature of Switzerland in summer and in winter. I would like to thank them for the good times and the warm support that I have received over the years. In particular, the time that I have spent with Jonathan, Aleix and Susana, Jonathan (Chuah), Sepideh, Mehrsa, Xinlin, Vasi, Pakshal, Alessio, Philip, Muze, Hongruyu and Zhiyuan has become a dear memory to me.

I would have not pulled through the many years of my studies without the unconditional support from my loving family. I would like to thank my parents and my brother, especially my mother, for always believing in me and taking care of me. I am thankful for the genuine support from my parents-in-law for always being there for me over the years.

Finally, I would like to dedicate my thesis to and share every success that I have achieved since 2018 with my husband, my best friend, Eric. It is safe to say that without his wholehearted support in my work or life, I would have not become who I am today. Eric was there for me when I was troubled by my studies, when I was in hospital, and when I was nervous in presentations. Eric was there for me when I moved from Zürich to Lausanne, when I struggled with work, and when I defended my thesis. Eric was there for me when I wanted to give up so many times. He is truly the hidden hero behind all my achievements and I have learned so much from him. Together as a team, we have moved mountains. I cannot find enough words to describe my gratitude for him.
