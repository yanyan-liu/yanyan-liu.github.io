Projects

(2024 - 2025) Data Analysis Workflow for Miniscope
In collaboration with the MibLab, ShanghaiTech University

  • Co-supervised a Master student on modeling and algorithms

  • Collaborated on deconvolution with spatially-varying PSFs for Miniscope

  • Participated in writing and reviewing the manuscript

(2022 - 2025) SWOPT: Novel Photo-Switching Photoacoustic Imaging Technology
Multi-year collaborative EU project hosted at Helmholtz Zentrum München

  • Co-led work package on data analysis workflow and inversion algorithms

  • Developed regularized temporal unmixing framework with GPU-acceleration

  • Technical reporting of deliverables and coordination between partners

(2023 - 2025) PSF Generator: Open-Source PyTorch Library for Point-Spread Function Computation
In collaboration with LCAV, EPFL

  • Unifying framework proving equivalence between different computational approaches

  • ML-ready implementation with comprehensive documentation and benchmarking

  • Napari plugin with user-friendly interface for 3D visualization

(2022 - 2024) Point-Spread-Function Engineering for MINFLUX Localization Microscopy
In collaboration with the Balzarotti Lab, Vienna BioCenter

  • Developed a differentiable optimization framework for MINFLUX microscopy

  • Investigated optimality of excitation patterns (donut and half-moon beams)

  • Enabled fast explorations of new beam patterns avoiding expensive experiments

(2020 - 2022) Artifact Correction for Optical Projection Tomography
In collaboration with the Idiap institute and the University of Applied Sciences of Western Switzerland

  • Systematic documentation of mechanical and optical artifacts for practitioners

  • Developed fast automatic calibration algorithms based on geometry and optics

  • Demonstrated effectiveness on improving the image quality on various samples

(2018 - 2019) Reconstruction and optimization of the illumination pattern in fluorescence molecular tomography
In collaboration with the Institute of Biomedical Engineering, ETH Zürich

  • Implemented a reconstruction algorithm with sparsity-based regularization for FMT

  • Developed an optimization approach for optimal design of the illumination pattern
