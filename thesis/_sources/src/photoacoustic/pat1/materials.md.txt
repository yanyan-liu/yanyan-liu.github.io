# Sample Preparation and Data Acquisition

## Reporters

We used three rsOAPs, ReBphP-PCM (Re), DrBphP-PCM (Dr) and RpBphP1-PCM (Rp),
in the experiments.
The protein was expressed in _E. coli_ BL21 (DE3) cells, induced with IPTG,
and purified using immobilized metal-affinity
chromatography followed by size-exclusion chromatography.
ON and OFF spectra of the protein samples were recorded using a spectrophotometer (Shimadzu UV-1800, Shimadzu Inc.,
Kyoto, Japan) after being illuminated with 660 nm or 780 nm LEDs (Thorlabs) in a
10 mm-pathlength quartz cuvette.

In {numref}`tab:pat1-reporters`, we show key photo-physical properties of the three rsOAPs,
including halftime of the OFF-switching cycle
{math}`\tau_{\text{OFF}}`, kinetic constant {math}`\bar{k}` derived based on {math}`\tau_{\text{OFF}}`, and
molar extinction coefficients {math}`\varepsilon^{\text{ON}}_{\text{770nm}}` and {math}`
\varepsilon^{\text{OFF}}_{\text{770nm}}` of ON and OFF states, respectively, at 770 nm (OFF-switching wavelength).
For a summary of complete properties of these reporters, refer to {cite:p}`mishra_multiplexed_2020`.

## Tissue-Mimicking Phantoms

For section [](#pat1-result-experimental-phantom), we prepared
tissue-mimicking cylindrical phantoms (used in ) of diameter 27 mm by
mixing 3\% intralipid
(Sigma-Aldrich, St. Louis, Missouri, USA), 3\% sheep blood (Thermo Scientific, Massachusetts, USA), and 2\% agarose
(Carl Roth, Karlsruhe, Germany) in a 50 ml flacon (30 mm diameter) as cast.
The mixture aims to replicate the optical properties of biological tissues, with intralipid serving as scatterer,
sheep blood as absorber, and agarose providing a gel matrix.
Protein samples were loaded into 580 {math}`\mu`m catheter tubing and positioned at different locations within the phantom.

We prepared two phantoms.
In the first phantom, we inserted four tubes of protein solutions with the following setting

- tube one: one portion of Re (4.23 {math}`\mu`M)
- tube two: one portion of Dr (3.71 {math}`\mu`M)
- tube three: 1/3 of one portion of Re mixed with 2/3 of one portion of Dr
- tube four: 2/3 of one portion of Re mixed with 1/3 of one portion of Dr

In the second phantom, we inserted three tubes of protein solutions

- tube one and two: each containing one portion of Re
- tube three: one portion of Dr

Tube one and two in the second phantom are placed at different depths from the boundary of the phantom
to test the impact of local fluence.

## Bead Phantom and Mouse Model

For section [](#pat1-result-experimental-mouse), we used a phantom made of 1mm-alginate beads inserted at random locations,
each bead containing _E. coli_ that express one of the three rsOAPs.
For the mouse model, Jurkat T cells and _E. coli_ expressing each of the three rsOAPs are implanted into a
4T1 tumor on the back of a mouse on the same plane.
More details can be found in {cite:p}`mishra_multiplexed_2020`.

## Acquisition

Samples were scanned using MSOT InVision 256-TF (iThera Medical GmbH, Munich, Germany), described in more detail in
{cite:p}`mishra_multiplexed_2020` with a protocol of 50 pulses at 680 nm and 50 pulses at 770 nm.
The pulse train was repeated 50 times, and an average was computed to reduce noise.
The acquired data were reconstructed using the ViewMSOT software version 4.0.2.2 (iThera Medical GmbH, Munich, Germany)
with the following settings: 100 {math}`\mu`m resolution; 50 kHz to 6.5 MHz filtering; and a speed-of-sound trim of
{math}`(-30) \text{m}/\text{s}`.
