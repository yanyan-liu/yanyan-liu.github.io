# Summary

We presented a comprehensive model for photo-switching photoacoustic mesoscopy and a global inversion framework
to reconstruct concentration maps directly from the acoustic measurements.
Our global framework includes a one-step reconstruction algorithm with a tailored {math}`\ell_1` regularization combined
with TV regularization on two spaces to mitigate noise and improve the unmixing quality.
We demonstrated that our regularized one-step approach was consistently more robust as compared to other approaches and
under various setups.
We provided an efficient GPU implementation of the pipeline.
Its benchmarking results underlines its importance for fast iterative-reconstruction algorithms.

Our framework is extendable to 3D imaging and flexible to include other models of the transducer impulse response.
It provides a unique opportunity for in-depth imaging at cellular resolution using photo-switching photoacoustic
mesoscopy.
The algorithm that has been developed will be tested on experimental data when they have been acquired on the
corresponding setup that is currently under development at the Helmholtz Zentrum Müchen.
