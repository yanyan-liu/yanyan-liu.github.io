# Modeling and Solving of Inverse Problems

Many tasks in different biomedical imaging modalities can be handled through the resolution of inverse
problems.
While each task presents distinct challenges,
the underlying approach to modeling and solving these problems share common principles.
This chapter introduces the general methodology and core mathematical concepts relevant to this thesis.
