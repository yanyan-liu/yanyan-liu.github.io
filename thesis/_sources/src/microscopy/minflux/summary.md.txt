# Summary

We introduced a computational approach to investigate the optimality of excitation patterns in MINFLUX,
a novel SMLM technique.
Our study leads to two key findings in the PSF engineering of MINFLUX.
The donut beam is optimal when the excitation pattern is constrained to take a single shape,
while the half-moon beams yield lower CRB when multiple shapes are available.
These results, obtained through our PSF-engineering pipeline, were further supported by
a mathematical investigation of the maximization of the gradient of the electric field.
Our approach is fast and economic to discover and investigate novel beam shapes for MINFLUX.
Additionally, this flexible framework can be extended to optimize PSFs for 3D
localization using vectorial models of the electric field propagation,
potentially expanding the capabilities of MINFLUX across diverse imaging scenarios.
